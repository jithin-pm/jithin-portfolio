Teresa Melvin

Teresa Melvin is a teenage digital artist, character designer, and global speaker whose bold, story-driven work inspires creativity in all ages. Named Creator of the Year by MET AMS, she has collaborated with CoinCoffee, United by Design, HUG, REMX, Make-A-Wish Foundation, NFT.NYC, LiLCreators, and more. Her art has lit up Times Square billboards and been exhibited in galleries across the USA, UK, Brazil, Dubai, Bali, India, and Canada.


She has spoken at 12+ international events, including NFT.NYC, Art Basel, and Adobe Summit, encouraging young artists to explore art and technology. Through her YouTube series Art ’n’ Chat, she connects with creatives worldwide to share stories and ideas.


Her mission: connect, learn, and inspire, proving creativity drives change.
